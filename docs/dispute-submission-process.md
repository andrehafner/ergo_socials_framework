# Formal Dispute Submission Process

---

## Contact Information

**Email:** team@sigmanauts.com

**Subject:** `Dispute – [Username] – [Social Platform]`

---

## What to Include

- Your username(s)
- Platform(s) involved
- Brief description of the issue
- Relevant dates or links (if applicable)
- Desired outcome (optional)

---

## Process

1. The dispute will be reviewed by the full Sigmanauts admin team.
2. Any admins directly involved will recuse themselves.
3. You may be invited into a private discussion for clarification.
4. A written decision will be issued within 10 business days.
5. Decisions are binding unless materially new evidence emerges.

---

## Important

- Please do not pursue the dispute publicly while under review.
- This process exists to ensure fairness, consistency, and community safety.
